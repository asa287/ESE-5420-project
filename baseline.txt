# Are News Popularity Predictors Stable Across Resampled Training Data?

## Group
Deepsick (Canvas group number: 1026513); Samantha, Crystal, Linglan.

## Changes since the proposal
We increased expanding-window CV from three to five folds to choose the Lasso penalty more reliably. We added a training-mean predictor to check whether Lasso improves on a simple reference and ran 20 seven-day block-bootstrap refits to check the selection pipeline before scaling to 200. We set aside a development holdout to estimate checkpoint error after tuning and preserve the final test for the end. We defined the future Elastic Net minus Lasso MAE comparison in advance: a paired interval with upper endpoint at or below zero supports no increase. An interval spanning zero is inconclusive.

## Data
Loaded the official UCI CSV: 39,644 articles, 58 candidate inputs; retain 44. Output: log(1 + shares). Exclude nine keyword and three reference-share statistics, redundant is_weekend and reference-category weekday_is_sunday; URL is excluded and timedelta only orders days. Drop no rows. Fixed rule: lexical ratios outside [0,1] become missing, then training-only median imputation. No such anomalies occur in development.

## Split
The final test set is the latest 145 observed publication days (9,112 articles, 2014-08-09 through 2014-12-31). We reserve it until all model and evaluation choices are frozen, then evaluate it once. The earlier 579 days comprise 483 days for training and tuning (24,635 articles) and a 96-day checkpoint holdout (5,897 articles, 2014-05-05 through 2014-08-08). The five expanding-window folds keep whole days together in chronological order. Bootstrap seeds are 542 for evaluation and 543 for refits.

## Baseline
Lasso with fold-local median imputation and standardization. Tune alpha over 20 logarithmically spaced values from 0.0001 to 1, minimizing mean fold MAE. Selected alpha: 0.00069519. Refit on all 24,635 training articles.

## Result
Checkpoint MAE: 0.5955, 95% interval [0.5762, 0.6066]; training-mean MAE: 0.6572, [0.6362, 0.6694]. Intervals use 2,000 seven-calendar-day moving-block bootstrap draws of checkpoint losses, conditional on fitted models. Lasso-minus-mean MAE: -0.0617, paired interval [-0.0682, -0.0533]. Selected-alpha tuning-fold MAEs: 0.6718, 0.7690, 0.6979, 0.7249, 0.6301; mean 0.6987, SD 0.0526 (descriptive spread, not an independent-fold SE). Training-mean CV MAE: 0.7357. Twenty fixed-alpha training refits select 37-42 inputs; selection frequencies appear in baseline.png.

## Issues found
The UCI page's instance count differs from the downloaded CSV. Lasso is fairly dense. Share exposure time and upstream topic-feature construction limit interpretation. Real-data stability does not establish a true feature list or causation.
